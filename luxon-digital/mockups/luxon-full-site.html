About Luxon

What does Luxon Digital do?

We help limo and private transportation companies use AI automation to capture more leads, respond faster, follow up automatically, generate reviews and cut down on repetitive admin work.

What is Luxon Limo AI?

Our done-for-you AI system built specifically for limo, black-car, chauffeur and private transportation businesses.

Does it replace my staff?

No. It supports your team by handling repetitive communication and covering calls when people are unavailable. A person is always available when needed.

Does it replace Moovs or Limo Anywhere?

No. Luxon works alongside your booking and dispatch software. You keep the tools you already use.

How the AI works

Can it answer my business phone?

Yes. The AI receptionist can handle approved inbound inquiries and collect trip information, around the clock if you choose.

Can it book rides?

It collects the booking information and supports your booking process. Final booking depends on your existing software and policies.

Can it give customers prices?

Only when you have approved pricing rules. It never makes up a price. Otherwise it collects the trip details for your team to quote.

What if it doesn't know the answer?

It doesn't guess. It collects the details, lets the customer know your team will help, and passes the conversation to a person.

What if a customer wants a person?

It honors the request right away and follows your handoff rules.

What if a customer is upset, or a driver hasn't arrived?

Those conversations go straight to your team. The AI never argues, makes promises or guesses a driver's location.

Setup & support

How long does setup take?

It depends on your requirements, phone setup and business information. You'll get a clear timeline during onboarding.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. Luxon is done for you. We handle the setup and provide ongoing support.

Will I approve what it says?

Yes. You review your business information, customer-facing answers and handoff rules before anything goes live.

What happens after I sign up?

Onboarding, then we build your system, test it, make any fixes, get your approval, launch, and keep improving it with you.

Pricing

A one-time setup fee plus a monthly service fee, based on the features and usage you need. Custom work is quoted separately. See the Pricing page for details.
