Questions

About follow-up

Will customers get too many messages?

No. Follow-up is short and spaced out, and it stops as soon as they respond or opt out.

Can I change what the messages say?

Yes. You approve every message, and we update them whenever your offers or policies change.

What if someone opts out?

Marketing messages stop right away, and the request is honored.
