Questions

About the AI Receptionist

Will it sound robotic?

Hear it for yourself in a live demo. It's friendly, professional and speaks in your company's name.

Can it give customers prices?

Only when you've approved pricing rules. Otherwise it collects the trip details and your team sends the quote.

Will it replace my staff?

No. It supports your team by covering the calls they can't get to.

What happens when it doesn't know an answer?

It doesn't guess. It collects the details, tells the customer your team will help, and hands the conversation to a person.
