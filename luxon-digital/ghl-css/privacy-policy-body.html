1. Who we are

Luxon Digital ("Luxon Digital", "we", "us" or "our") provides done-for-you AI services for limousine, black-car and chauffeur companies, including Luxon Limo AI, an AI receptionist, automated lead follow-up, review requests and lead management.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect information when you visit our website, book a demo, contact us, receive calls or text messages from us, or use our services. By using our website or services, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: your name, company name, phone number and email address.
  • Booking details: the date and time you choose for a demo, and anything you share in a booking or contact form.
  • Business information: details you provide during onboarding, such as your services, service areas, hours, FAQs, policies and approved answers.
  • Messages: what you send us by phone, text, email or chat.
  • Billing details: payment information is handled by our payment processor. We do not store full card numbers.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, IP address, pages visited, the site that referred you, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Information from cookies and similar tools, explained in section 6.

3. How we use your information

  • To answer your questions and schedule and run your demo.
  • To set up, provide, support and improve our services.
  • To send appointment reminders, service updates and, if you agreed to receive them, marketing messages.
  • To send invoices and manage your account.
  • To understand how our website is used and to measure our advertising.
  • To protect our business, prevent misuse, and meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Calls, texts & AI

Our services answer calls and messages for our clients (the limo and chauffeur companies we work with) using AI. When a rider or customer contacts one of our clients:

  • The AI may answer the call or message, collect trip details such as name, phone number, pickup, drop-off, date and number of passengers, and pass those details to the client's team.
  • Calls and messages may be recorded or transcribed so the client can review them and so the service can be checked and improved. Where the law requires it, callers are told about recording.
  • The AI works only from information the client has approved. It does not make up prices or promises, and it hands the conversation to a person when it is unsure.

Information collected for a client belongs to that client, and we handle it on the client's behalf and under their instructions. If you are a rider or customer of one of our clients, please contact that company directly about your information. We will help them respond to your request.

5. Text message (SMS) consent

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive text messages, we may text you about your inquiry, demo appointments, reminders, account and service updates and, if you agreed, offers from Luxon Digital.

  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help.
  • Agreeing to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the categories of sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

6. Cookies & analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar tools to keep the site working, remember your preferences, understand how visitors use the site, and measure our advertising, including on Google. These tools may collect your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work properly without them. You can manage how Google uses your information for ads at adssettings.google.com.

7. How we share information

We share information only as needed to run our business and provide our services:

  • Service providers who help us operate, such as website and booking hosting, phone and text delivery, AI and transcription, email, payment processing and analytics. They may use the information only to provide services to us.
  • Our clients, for calls and messages handled on their behalf.
  • Legal reasons, when required by law or to protect our rights, our clients, users or the public.
  • Business changes, such as a merger or sale, where the new owner keeps this policy's protections.
  • With your permission, in any other case.

Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties, as explained in section 5.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information, including access controls and trusted service providers. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. If you believe your information has been put at risk, please contact us right away.

9. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy: to provide our services, keep business and tax records, settle disputes and meet legal obligations. Information we hold for a client is kept or deleted according to that client's instructions and our agreement with them. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

10. Your choices & rights

  • Texts: reply STOP to any message.
  • Emails: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Access, correction and deletion: ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under your state's privacy laws, such as the right to know what we collect, and to opt out of targeted advertising. To make a request, contact us using the details in section 13. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will not treat you differently for using your rights.

11. Children's privacy

Our website and services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.
